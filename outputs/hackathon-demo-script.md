# LearnSphere AI — two-minute demo script

## 0:00–0:15 — Start with the problem

“Most AI study tools understand documents. LearnSphere understands the learner. That matters because a student can study for hours and still carry one misconception that blocks everything else.”

## 0:15–0:35 — Show the Learning Profile

Open the dashboard. Point to topic mastery, the confidence trend, and the recommended next action.

“This is not a progress tracker. Every answer changes this learning model: what Alex understands, how confident Alex is, and where their reasoning may have gone wrong.”

## 0:35–0:55 — Show material grounding

Upload the supplied science PDF.

“LearnSphere extracts the PDF into page-aware learning chunks. Its answers, notes, and quizzes are grounded in the student’s material—not a generic web answer.”

Ask: “Why do seasons occur?” Show the source badges.

## 0:55–1:30 — The wow moment

Submit the wrong answer: “Earth is closer to the Sun in summer,” with high confidence.

“A normal quiz says wrong. LearnSphere detects *why* the student said it: they are using a distance-to-Sun mental model. It explains why that feels intuitive, corrects it with axial tilt and sunlight angle, and asks one verification question.”

Show the misconception card and profile refresh.

## 1:30–1:50 — Explain the feedback loop

“That insight updates the next action. The system doesn’t just create content—it chooses what Alex needs to repair next. Each quiz makes the future explanation more personal.”

## 1:50–2:00 — Close

“LearnSphere doesn’t just understand documents. It understands learners.”

## Demo safeguards

- Keep the demo PDF local and already selected.
- Confirm `LEARN_SPHERE_GEMINI_API_KEY` and the embedding model have warmed up before presenting.
- If generation is slow, open the seeded misconception insight while explaining the architecture; never wait silently.
- Do not demo roadmap features such as gamification or voice mode.
